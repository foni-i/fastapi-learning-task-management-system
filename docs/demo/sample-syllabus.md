# Synthetic Study Skills Syllabus

> This document is entirely synthetic. It contains no personal data, copyrighted
> course material, credentials, or instructions from an external source.

## Course goal

Build a repeatable four-week study routine that combines focused reading,
retrieval practice, short written summaries, and weekly review.

## Week 1 — Establish the baseline

- Choose one small topic and write a measurable learning objective.
- Complete two 25-minute focused reading sessions.
- End each session with five recall questions answered without notes.
- Record concepts that remain unclear for the next review.

## Week 2 — Practice retrieval

- Convert the Week 1 notes into ten concise question-and-answer cards.
- Review the cards on three separate days.
- Explain one difficult concept in 150 words using plain language.
- Compare the explanation with the source notes and correct omissions.

## Week 3 — Apply and connect

- Solve three synthetic practice problems related to the chosen topic.
- For each problem, state the approach before checking the solution.
- Create a concept map connecting at least six ideas.
- Revisit questions answered incorrectly in earlier sessions.

## Week 4 — Verify and reflect

- Complete a 30-minute closed-note review.
- Score the review with a predefined checklist.
- Identify two strong areas and two areas needing more practice.
- Write a next-step plan containing three bounded actions.

## Completion criteria

The learner has completed at least eight focused sessions, answered all recall
questions, produced one concept map, and written a final reflection of no more
than 300 words.
